## How novels open

A source that declares novel content opens in the text reader automatically, so you never have to choose the right reader by hand.

## Reading comfort

Font size, line spacing and theme are adjustable, because a wall of text is not the same reading experience as a page of images.

## Remembering your place

The novel reader tracks your position in each chapter and returns you to it, the same way the image reader does.

## Reading aloud

Text to speech can read a chapter aloud using the voices installed on your device, which is useful on a commute or while doing something else.

## When a chapter is empty

A novel chapter that opens blank usually comes from a source that returned text in a shape the reader cannot show. Updating the extension is the first thing to try.

## Novels versus manga

A novel is text and a manga is images, so a source has to declare the right type for the content to open correctly. A wrong type sends it to the wrong reader.

## Cheat sheet

- Novels open in a text reader, chosen by content type.
- Font, spacing and theme are adjustable.
- Read-aloud uses the voices installed on your device.
- A blank chapter usually means the source returned unusable text.

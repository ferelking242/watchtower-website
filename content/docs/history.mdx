## What it records

Opening an episode or a chapter adds an entry with the time and how far you got. History is a record of activity, not a second library, so it fills up on its own as you use the app.

## Filtering and sorting

Narrow the list to today, this week or this month, switch between a list and a grid, and sort by date or title. These change what is shown and nothing else.

## Getting back to a series

Tap an entry to reopen the series where you left it. History is the fastest route back to something you sampled once and set aside.

## Incognito and privacy

While incognito mode is on, nothing new is recorded, so nothing you open appears in history. Turning it off resumes recording from that point.

## Clearing history

You can clear history without touching your library or your saved progress. Clearing it only removes the record of what you opened.

## When history looks wrong

An entry that shows as unwatched usually means the source changed the episode's address, so the app treats it as new. Removing the stray entry and refreshing the series tidies it up.

## Cheat sheet

- History records activity, it does not replace your library.
- Filters narrow the view without deleting anything.
- Incognito mode stops new history from being written.
- Clearing history leaves your progress untouched.

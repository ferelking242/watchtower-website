## What is counted

Totals per media type, the number of chapters or episodes, the time spent, and how your library splits across statuses.

## Time and activity

The time card estimates how long you have spent watching and reading, and the activity chart shows how that is spread over recent weeks.

## Top genres

The genres you actually spend time on, which is often not the ones you would guess. It is a good way to find a new series in a lane you already like.

## Per media type

Statistics are split by media type, so anime and manga totals are never mixed together.

## Where the numbers come from

Everything is computed from your own library and history on the device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## When it says there is no data

A brand new install has nothing to summarise yet. The figures appear once you have watched or read something and it has been recorded.

## Cheat sheet

- Statistics are computed on the device, from your own data.
- Totals are kept separate for each media type.
- The activity chart shows how reading and watching spread over time.
- A new install shows no data until something is recorded.

## Contract / example

```text
Overview     entries · chapters · hours
Breakdown    watching · completed · on hold · dropped
Charts       activity over time · top genres · downloads
```

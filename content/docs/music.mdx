## Where the music comes from

Music extensions provide tracks and collections, and a local library covers files you already own. Both behave like normal sources.

## Albums and artists

Music is browsed by album and artist as well as by track, which is what makes a music library usable rather than a long list of files.

## The player

A queue with shuffle and repeat, a now-playing sheet, and cover art. Playback continues while you move around the app.

## Lyrics

Tracks that carry lyrics show them in a sheet, and synced lyrics follow the position of the track where the source provides timing.

## Scrobbling

Connecting Last.fm records what you play to your profile, in the same way tracking records what you watch.

## When a track will not play

A music source that has stopped working behaves like any other broken source. Update the extension, and expect a service-based source to be unavailable while that service is down.

## Cheat sheet

- Music comes from extensions or from files you already own.
- Albums and artists are first-class, not just tracks.
- Last.fm scrobbling is available when you connect it.
- Lyrics follow the track when the source provides timing.

## Contract / example

```text
Music
├── Home       recent and recommended
├── Library    albums and artists you keep
├── Player     queue, shuffle, repeat
└── Lyrics     synced when the track has them

Connect      Last.fm scrobbling
```

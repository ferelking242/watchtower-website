## The month view

Days with releases are marked, and tapping a day jumps to that date's list. It answers what is out this week without opening a single series.

## The weekly schedule

The schedule lays out a week with the cover of each upcoming release, which reads better than a dense month grid on a phone.

## Filtering by status

Show only the statuses you care about, so completed and paused series do not clutter a view you use to plan the week.

## What the dates mean

A date is when the source last said something is expected, not a promise. Sources that do not publish dates simply do not appear.

## Indicating watched entries

An option marks what you have already seen on the schedule, so a week view doubles as a checklist.

## When the calendar is empty

An empty calendar usually means the sources you follow do not publish release dates. That is a limit of the source, not a fault in the app.

## Cheat sheet

- Release dates come from the sources, not from the app.
- The schedule can be filtered by series status.
- An option marks entries you have already watched.
- A source without dates will not appear at all.

## Contract / example

```text
Calendar      a month view of upcoming releases
Schedule      a week view with your statuses

Statuses   watching · planning · completed · paused
Options    week starts Monday · indicate watched
```

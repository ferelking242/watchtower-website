## The anime player

Episodes play in the same player as everything else, with quality, audio, subtitles and speed in the usual sheets.

## Skipping intros and endings

With intro skipping on, a button counts down during the opening and jumps past it, and the ending is handled the same way. It is a setting, so it can be turned off.

## Why a title cannot be matched

Skipping needs to identify the series and episode. A title that cannot be identified is simply played without the button, rather than failing.

## The skip timeout

A short countdown gives you time to cancel before the jump happens. Its length is configurable if the default feels rushed.

## Discovering anime

A discovery screen offers recommended, popular, upcoming, recently updated and top rated lists, plus genre rows, so you can find something without a source open.

## When discovery shows an error

The discovery rows come from an external service rather than your sources. When that service is unreachable the rest of the app is unaffected, and a source still works normally.

## Cheat sheet

- Intro skipping needs the series and episode to be identified.
- Skipping is a setting and can be turned off.
- The skip countdown length is configurable.
- Discovery uses an external service; sources work regardless.

## Contract / example

```text
Player      the same controls as any video
AniSkip     skip opening and ending automatically
Discovery   recommended, popular and by genre
```

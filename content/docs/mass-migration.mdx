## When to use it

A source that has stopped working, or one you want to move away from, is the case for mass migration. Migrating keeps your progress and your categories.

## Choosing source and destination

Pick the group of entries to move and the source to move them to. The app searches the destination for each title on its own.

## Reviewing the matches

Every match is shown before anything changes, so you can accept the good ones and skip the wrong ones. Matching by title is never perfect.

## Applying the change

Applying rewrites each entry to point at the new source and carries your progress across. Series with no match are left untouched.

## Reading the summary

The run ends with counts of what moved, what was skipped and what failed. A failed item is usually a title the destination does not carry.

## When nothing matches

If most titles fail to match, the destination is a poor fit and a different source is the better answer. Nothing is lost by stopping, because changes only happen when you apply.

## Cheat sheet

- Mass migration moves a group of entries in one pass.
- Every match is reviewed before anything is changed.
- Your progress and categories carry across.
- Nothing changes until you apply the results.

## Contract / example

```text
1. choose the broken source
2. pick the destination source
3. review each match
4. apply, then read the summary
```
